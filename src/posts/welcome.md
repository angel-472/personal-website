Hey, I'm Angel. I'm a self taught software engineer from Puerto Rico, and this little corner of the internet is where everything I build finally has a home. For a long time my projects lived scattered across GitHub repos, half finished folders on my laptop, and screenshots I sent to friends at 2am. This site is my attempt to pull all of that into one place.

You'll find two things here. The Projects tab is the stuff I've actually shipped, from small tools I built to scratch my own itch to bigger things like game engines and libraries. The Posts tab is where I write about the process, which is usually the more interesting part. Why I picked one approach over another, what broke, what I'd do differently, and the occasional monthly update on what I've been up to.

I'm not trying to make this a polished tech blog with a content calendar. It's more of a public notebook. If something I learned saves you an afternoon of debugging, that's a win. And if you want to talk about any of it, or you're working on something and think I could help, my email is right at the bottom of the page. Thanks for stopping by.
